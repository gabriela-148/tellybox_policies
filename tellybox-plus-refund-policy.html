Refund Policy

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for Tellybox Plus subscriptions purchased through Apple's App Store.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Purchased Tellybox Plus through Apple?

Apple handles billing and refund requests for App Store subscriptions. To request a refund, sign in to Apple's Report a Problem website using the Apple Account used for your purchase.

1. Subscription overview

Tellybox Plus is an auto-renewable monthly subscription that unlocks premium features in the Tellybox app. The listed U.S. price is $0.99 per month. Your actual price, currency, and any applicable taxes are shown by Apple before you confirm your purchase and may differ by App Store region.

Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it before the next renewal. Apple processes payments for subscriptions purchased through Apple's in-app purchase system.

2. How to request a refund

Refunds for Tellybox Plus purchases billed by Apple are requested through Apple. Apple reviews refund requests under its applicable policies, and eligibility may depend on your location, purchase circumstances, and applicable law. Submitting a request does not guarantee approval.

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used to buy Tellybox Plus.
  2. Choose “I'd like to” → “Request a refund.”
  3. Select your reason, then select the Tellybox Plus subscription purchase.
  4. Submit your request and follow any updates from Apple.

3. How to cancel Tellybox Plus

You may cancel future renewals through your Apple Account at any time. On an iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select Tellybox Plus.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription, if available.

Canceling stops future renewals. Ordinarily, your premium access continues through the end of your current paid subscription period unless Apple ends or revokes the entitlement sooner. Canceling does not automatically refund a charge that has already been processed.

To avoid a future charge, cancel before your next renewal. Deleting the Tellybox app or your Tellybox account does not, by itself, cancel an App Store subscription.

4. If your refund is approved

If Apple approves a refund, Apple determines how and when the refund is returned to your original payment method. Your access to Tellybox Plus features may end when Apple revokes the refunded subscription entitlement, even if a previously displayed renewal or expiration date has not yet passed.

If your request is not approved, you can review your options with Apple Support. Tellybox cannot guarantee or override Apple's refund decision for an Apple-billed purchase.

5. Charged but premium features aren't available?

If you purchased Tellybox Plus but premium access is missing, first make sure you're signed in to the Tellybox account you intended to use. Then try the app's Restore Purchases option, if available. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

If access still doesn't work, contact Tellybox using the support contact listed on its App Store page. For billing issues or an unrecognized Apple charge, contact Apple Support.

6. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable refund, cancellation, or consumer protection rights available to you under applicable law. Where local law provides additional rights, those rights apply.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to Tellybox Plus, App Store processes, or applicable requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision. Changes do not remove rights that applicable law provides for prior purchases.
