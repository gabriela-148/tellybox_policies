Privacy Policy for Tellybox

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Tellybox ("Tellybox," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share that information, and the choices available to you when you use the Tellybox mobile application.

Tellybox is a television show discovery, rating, and review application that allows users to create an account, rate and review television shows, interact with other users, and keep track of television content.

By using Tellybox, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you register or sign in using Tellybox's email-and-password login, Tellybox collects the email address and password you provide for account registration, authentication, and account recovery or security processes. Tellybox stores password-verification information as a one-way password hash, rather than storing a readable password in its account database.

If Google Sign-In is available and you choose it instead, Tellybox may receive your name, email address, and information needed to authenticate your Google account. Tellybox does not receive your Google password.

We may also store information associated with your Tellybox profile, such as your username, profile information, or profile picture if those features are available and you choose to provide them.

Ratings, Reviews, and User Activity

When you use Tellybox, we collect information that you choose to create or provide through the app.

This information may include:

  • TV show ratings
  • Written TV show reviews
  • Users you follow
  • Ratings and reviews associated with your Tellybox account
  • Other interactions you make through Tellybox's social features

Ratings, reviews, usernames, profile information, and certain social activity may be visible to other Tellybox users as part of the app's social and review features.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in public reviews or other publicly visible content.

Push Notification Information

If you enable push notifications, Tellybox may receive and store a device push notification token associated with your device.

This token allows us to send notifications to your device. Push notification tokens are used for notification delivery and are not intended to personally identify you outside of the Tellybox service.

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected through Tellybox to:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain your account
  • Provide your Tellybox profile
  • Save your television show ratings and reviews
  • Save information about users you follow
  • Display content you choose to share with other users
  • Provide the core features and functionality of Tellybox
  • Deliver push notifications you have enabled
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service
  • Detect spam, abusive content, or inappropriate activity
  • Moderate user-generated content
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Reviews and Content Moderation

Tellybox uses automated filtering to help identify potentially inappropriate, abusive, harmful, offensive, or spam content submitted through reviews and other user-generated content features.

Content submitted to Tellybox may therefore be automatically analyzed for moderation purposes.

Content that violates Tellybox's rules, community standards, or other applicable policies may be blocked, restricted, flagged, or removed.

Automated filtering may not identify every instance of inappropriate content and may occasionally identify acceptable content incorrectly.

4. Account Sign-In

Tellybox supports email-and-password authentication. When you use this option, the email address you provide identifies your Tellybox account; your password is processed to verify sign-in and is not displayed to other users. Never include passwords in public reviews or support messages.

If Google Sign-In remains available, you may choose it instead of Tellybox's email-and-password sign-in.

When you choose to sign in using Google, Google processes information as part of the authentication process and may provide Tellybox with the information needed to create or access your account, including your name and email address.

Tellybox does not receive your Google account password.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

5. Push Notifications

Tellybox may use Apple's push notification services to deliver notifications to iOS devices.

When notifications are enabled, a device token may be generated and associated with your Tellybox account or device so that notifications can be delivered.

Tellybox may use push notifications for features such as:

  • New follower activity
  • Activity involving your reviews
  • Account-related notifications
  • Other relevant Tellybox app updates

You can control whether Tellybox sends notifications through your iPhone or iPad notification settings.

6. Television Show Information and TMDB

Tellybox may use third-party television data services, including The Movie Database ("TMDB"), to provide information about television shows.

This information may include:

  • TV show names
  • Show descriptions
  • Poster and backdrop artwork
  • Release information
  • Episode information
  • Cast or other entertainment metadata

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms governing their collection and processing of information.

Tellybox is not affiliated with or endorsed by TMDB unless otherwise specifically stated.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or process information with third-party service providers when reasonably necessary to operate Tellybox.

These services may include providers used for:

  • User authentication
  • Application hosting
  • Database hosting
  • Push notification delivery
  • Television show information
  • Security
  • Content moderation
  • Technical infrastructure

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services and in accordance with their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or legal processes
  • Respond to lawful governmental requests
  • Enforce our policies
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Protect the rights, safety, or property of Tellybox, our users, or others

8. Public Information

Tellybox includes social and user-generated content features.

Information that you intentionally make available through these features may be visible to other users.

This may include:

  • Your username
  • Your profile information
  • Your profile picture, if applicable
  • Your television show ratings
  • Your written reviews
  • Users you follow
  • Other content you choose to share publicly through Tellybox

You should consider what information you choose to include in publicly visible content.

9. Data Retention

We retain account information and associated content for as long as your Tellybox account remains active or as reasonably necessary to provide the service.

We may also retain certain information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Protect the security of Tellybox and its users
  • Enforce our agreements or policies

When an account is deleted, personal information associated with the account will be deleted or de-identified where reasonably possible, except for information we are required or permitted to retain for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.

10. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Tellybox account and associated personal information.

Tellybox provides an account deletion option within the application.

When you delete your account, Tellybox will delete or de-identify the personal information and user content associated with your account that is no longer necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational purposes.

Deleting your Tellybox account does not delete your Google account.

If you experience an issue deleting your account, you may contact us using the contact information provided at the bottom of this Privacy Policy.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These rights may include the ability to:

  • Request access to information associated with your account
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information
  • Obtain information about how your information is used
  • Withdraw certain permissions where applicable

You may also control certain information and permissions directly through Tellybox or your device settings.

For example, you can disable Tellybox push notifications through your device's Settings application.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information associated with Tellybox accounts.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet, mobile application, database, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We therefore cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

13. Third-Party Services

Tellybox depends on certain third-party services to provide its functionality.

These may include services provided by:

  • Google, if you choose Google Sign-In for account authentication
  • Apple, for iOS functionality and push notification delivery
  • TMDB, for television show information and artwork
  • Hosting and database providers used to operate Tellybox's backend services

These third parties maintain their own privacy policies and may independently collect or process information when you interact with their services.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of applicable third-party services to understand their practices.

14. Children's Privacy

Tellybox does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Tellybox in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Tellybox, our data practices, legal requirements, or operational needs.

When we make changes, we will update the Effective Date shown at the top of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy for updates.
